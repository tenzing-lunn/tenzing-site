Meditate With Me

T. Lunn · Freelance Developer · Aug 2026 – Present

Abstract: Client work, live since 1 September 2026.

A warm screen asks one thing at a time. Your name, where you're from, with others or alone, how long, which bell, any sound. Then a bowl. Strike it and the light goes down, and you're sitting in front of the earth, where everyone sitting this hour is a candle and one line tells you who is with you. Choose with others and you end on the same bell, at the same second, as everyone else who did.

CITE AS

Why I built it

Sitting alone and sitting together are different things, and almost every meditation app quietly gives you the first while implying the second. The whole product is one promise: the bell lands at the same instant for everyone who chose to share it. Everything else is in service of that.

How it works

There is no scheduler. A session exists for every hour of the day whether or not a row exists for it, so the code floors the clock to the hour, looks for an override, and falls back to an ambient hour when it finds none. No cron, no queue, nothing to fail at three in the morning.

Re-evaluation

v1 treated the arXiv format as the fluff. It isn't: the datelines, category codes, cite-as lines and listing rows are the personality, and DESIGN.md asks for them. What actually makes the page hard to read is saying the same thing twice, widgets that contradict themselves and empty states. v2 cuts only those.
